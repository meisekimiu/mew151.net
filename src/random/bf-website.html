Why is there a random rectangle on the Random Stuff section of my website that says My favorite food is pizza.?


My favorite food is pizza.

Yeah, that thing!

Well, like most of the random images and stuff after the main directory listing, it's some weird obscure nostalgic personal reference. In this case, it's pulled from a website I made during my first year of high school in a "business foundations" class.

The assignment, as I remember it many years later anyways, was to make a little personal website that had various things on it. We were to include fun facts about ourselves, our interests and hobbies, and our goals and quotations that we believe in. We also needed 3 images on each page related to the page contents, which is where the box with a pizza clip art originated. I guess those all make you look like an accomplished business person or something.

Anyway, at the time we were instructed to make the website in Microsoft Publisher, but since I knew HTML at the time I decided to write my website from scratch. I was a bit snarky about this on the actual website.

I've touched up the site, updating deadnames and redacting some personal details, so you can browse it at the following link if you're really curious what 14-year-old me was like:

Enter The Site
