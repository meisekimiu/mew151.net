Site History

As a child I was fascinated by the idea of having my own website. There was something so magical about being a webmaster, slowly developing a website filled with interesting content. And so of course I started learning how to make my own website while relatively young.

Traces of what would eventually become this very site you're browsing now first started development in 2008. It's gone through a few iterations, visions, and redesigns since then, so I thought it'd be fun to have a section of my site dedicated to the site's evolution.

As I write this, a new redesign of my site will soon be launching (as you read this, it has launched!). As I continue to redesign my site over time, I hope to keep the old designs alive in some way through this "living history" of Mew151.net!
