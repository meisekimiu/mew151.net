Pokemon - Censored Version


Version 1.0 Unrevised

Ahh... the world of pokemon. I, myself, love watching the anime, but there's one thing about the anime. One thing about the entire series. I'll tell you what it is: Censorship. Some things, don't have a reason to be censored! Other times, your jaw will drop. First off, the infamous banned episodes.

The banned episodes... some are bad. Some, are good. Some are seizure inducing. The first on the list, is "Legend of Dratini." Legend of Dratini was banned for puns. Er... I mean... GUNS (althogh, bad puns are lethal, too!)!!! Yes, guns. It starts off with Ash, Misty, and Brock walking towards the safari zone. Ash is singing badly, and soon Brock starts singing (screaming). The Safari zone manager bursts through the door, and commands them to stop the bad singing, or he'll shoot them! Yes, bad start. I'll just stop there. Next one is, "beauty at the beach." er.... wait, never mind. You can google that one (make sure you have sunglasses on...). The real next one is, "Electric soldier porygon." Here's what happens: Porygon gets warped to cyberspace. Ash and friends, chase after porygon. Team rocket shoots missle. Pikachu thunderbolts it, creates blinking lights, and causes more than 400 epileptic seizures accross Japan. Not a good ending, ne? Well, that's why porygon hasn't been shown in the pokemon anime at all since that. I don't understand since it was pikachu who caused the seizures. I guess it's too cute.

The forbidden words... death, die, died, dead. For some reason, pokemon never die. I know, there's pokemon tower. I know, Lucario died. I know, Celebi died and then came back to life. But, do you ever see a pokemon really... die??? Do you see pokemon eat other pokemon? In the pokedex, it references it, but you never see it. In fact, in one episode, Max orders a cheeseburger (english dub). If he really ate the cheesburger, wouldn't he be eating a tauros, or miltank? Another unsolved mystery of Pokemon.

I guess this article comes to an end... except for this last banned thing. Remember in Advanced Generation, Moroson, the one with the sandwich? In Japan, it was a giant onigiri (rice ball). They changed it into a sandwich in the english dub! Who cares? Little american kids probably think it's a doughnut. So, now you know, the totally banned things from pokemon. Cya later!
