www.freewebs.com review

Many people want a free website. I know I do, and that's why I picked freewebs. Their name says it all, Free websites. You can make your own website, build it with a WYSIWYG editor, and everything! You need NO prior knowledge requiring html, javascript, css, php, asp, and anything else. But what if you want to make your website entirely out of html? That's what I wanted. You can do that. It is awesomeness. I bet I could find a better host, but this is one of the places that gives you like, 150 mb, which is more than enough needed to set up a basic website. Now, premium services is the only thing they need to work on... it's $8 for only 750 stupid megabytes. I can pay $6 a month for 2500 gigabytes at lunarpages. Plus with lunarpages, I'd get my own domain, and php! However, I don't have any money. But, I'll save money for the day I'll finally get a lunarpages site. For now, I'll go with freewebs. Hey!
