The "Shinymew" Era - Site History

After messing around with Freewebs, I yearned for more. And so I started developing a new fancy dynamic website powered by PHP and MySQL. Inspired by browser games like Neopets, I decided to make a Pokémon-themed roleplaying website that let you actually take care of Pokémon, battle with other trainers, shop for items, and more.

I saved up some money, and after showcasing a super early prototype of the website to my parents, somehow convinced them to allow me to buy a paid web hosting plan and domain name. Inspired by the website DS Ultimate.net I wanted the domain Mew151.net, because .net domains seemed cool and uncommon at the time. I think these days I'd probably try to get the browser game nearly finished before I set up the domain and hosting, but I was just so excited that I couldn't wait.

And so, for its first few years, the website at Mew151.net was a landing page for the upcoming roleplaying experience I was developing up. I posted occasional development updates with screenshots of my progress. I think I even tried to promote my site to a major Pokémon fan website at the time, but they rejected it since it was just a landing page.

Ah yes, the "I'm still working on this" update that always happens right before the developer stops working on it forever.

The site was pretty bare, but I was hopeful that it'd grow into something big over time. ... It did not. I think by 2010 the project was mostly abandoned, though I still liked having a domain and web hosting. So I kept it up and enjoyed the benefits of hosting my own images and files to link to on forums and chatrooms.

One page I'm sad to say I don't have an archived copy of is the "About the Administrator" page. I don't have any memories of it, but I assume at one point I was so ashamed of it that I deleted any local copies I had. Unfortunately, the Internet Archive does not have a backup either. I'd love to see what was on that page even if any pronouns caused me pain.

Speaking of shame, at some point I grew old enough to enter high school and well, the thing about being a teenager is that you start to feel ashamed and embarrassed about the things that you liked only a few years ago as a child. So at some point in high school, I commented out the old update blog and left Mew151.net as a blank landing page. But that's for next chapter.

By the way, the internal codename I gave to this browser game was Shinymew, which is why this section is called that.
